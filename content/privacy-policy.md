## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **CSRGO Wishes** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website [wishes.csrgo.com](https://wishes.csrgo.com) or use any of our wish-template services.

By accessing or using CSRGO Wishes, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our site.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use our templates or place an order, you may voluntarily provide:

- **Sender & recipient names** — used to personalise the wish page.
- **Gender selection** — used solely to select appropriate illustrations.
- **Custom messages** — entered to appear on the wish card.
- **Contact details** — such as your WhatsApp number, if you initiate contact with us.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the site, we may automatically collect:

- **Browser & device type** — for compatibility optimisation.
- **IP address** — for security and analytics.
- **Pages visited and time spent** — via privacy-respecting analytics tools.
- **Referring URL** — to understand how visitors discover us.

### 2.3 Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser **localStorage** only to remember your light/dark theme preference. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Generate and serve personalised wish pages from your inputs.
- Respond to your enquiries and fulfil orders.
- Improve the quality, design, and performance of our templates.
- Ensure the security and integrity of our platform.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

- **Service providers** — trusted tools (e.g., hosting, analytics) that assist us in operating our website, under strict confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal requirements** — when required to comply with a legal obligation, protect rights, or prevent harm.
- **Business transfers** — in the event of a merger or acquisition, user data may be transferred with prior notice.

---

## 5. Data Retention

Personalised wish-page data (names, messages) is stored only as long as the wish link remains active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

---

## 6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures — including HTTPS encryption and secure hosting — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of **13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us immediately.
