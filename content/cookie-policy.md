## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites to remember information about your visit. They are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, and provide anonymous usage statistics.

---

## 2. Our Cookie Philosophy

At CSRGO Wishes, we believe in a **privacy-first** approach. We keep our use of cookies and browser storage to an absolute minimum. We do **not** use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or sell any cookie-based data to third parties.

---

## 3. What We Store

### 3.1 localStorage (not a cookie, but similar)

We use your browser's **localStorage** — a client-side storage mechanism — to remember a single preference:

| Key | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| `theme` | Stores your chosen colour scheme (`light` or `dark`) so it persists between visits. | Until you clear browser storage |

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.

### 3.2 Session Behaviour

Interactive wish pages (e.g., the Friendship Day game) use **in-memory JavaScript state** only. No game progress, stage data, or user inputs are stored in cookies or localStorage beyond your active session.

## 5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools (that do not set persistent cookies) to understand aggregate traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is collected via analytics.

---

## 6. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites (including ours). For guidance on managing cookies, visit your browser's help section:

- **Chrome** — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
- **Firefox** — Settings → Privacy & Security
- **Safari** — Preferences → Privacy
- **Edge** — Settings → Cookies and site permissions
